Welcome to Etha Kallu

A simple place to learn about local palm traditions, the trees, the people and the heritage that connects them.

🌴 Palm Tradition

Learn about the traditional process of collecting sap from local palmyra and palm trees.

🌿 Natural Setting

Enjoy a calm rural atmosphere surrounded by the landscape and traditions of Kalvacherla.

🏡 Local Heritage

Experience a small part of Telangana's living rural culture and generations-old practices.

Our Tradition

Palm-tapping is a long-standing rural tradition. At Etha Kallu, we present this heritage in a simple, respectful and responsible way, with respect for the trees, land and local community.
